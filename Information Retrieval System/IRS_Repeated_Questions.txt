1. "Describe the process of creating an inverted index with an example. How can this process be optimized using block addressing?" (Nov 2025 Q.4a, Jun 2024 Q.5b, Jun 2023 Q.5a)
2. "What is inverted Index? Describe the process of creating an inverted index without stop words for the following example: 'This is a text. A text has many words. Words are made from letters.'" (Dec 2024 Q.4b)

What Examiners Look For:
  • Definition: Mapping from terms/words to the documents (and positions) in which they occur.
  • Components: Vocabulary/Dictionary + Postings List (DocID, Term Frequency, Token Positions).

TOPIC: User Relevance Feedback (Rocchio Algorithm & Feedback Models)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Marks: 10 Marks (and 5M)
Total Appearances: 4 Times
Appeared in:
  • NOV 2025 (Q.6a - 10M)
  • 14/12/2024 (Q.6a - 5M)
  • JUNE 2024 (Q.2a - 10M)
  • 22/06/2023 (Q.2a - 10M)

Exact Question Variations:
"Explain Relevance Feedback. Discuss how a user relevance feedback model can be applied to improve search results in search recommendation engine." (Jun 2024 Q.2a, Jun 2023 Q.2a, Nov 2025 Q.6a)

What Examiners Look For:
  • Concept: Iterative query reformulation based on user marking of relevant and non-relevant documents.
  • Rocchio Formula:
      q_m = (alpha * q_0) + (beta * 1/|D_r| * sum_{d in D_r} d) - (gamma * 1/|D_{nr}| * sum_{d in D_{nr}} d)
      where alpha = original query weight, beta = relevant doc weight, gamma = non-relevant doc weight.
  • Feedback Types: Explicit feedback, Implicit feedback (clicks, dwell time), Pseudo-relevance (blind) feedback.

  • Suffix Trie: Branching tree where each edge represents a single character ending in string terminator $.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NUMERICAL TYPE 1: VECTOR SPACE MODEL TF-IDF COSINE SIMILARITY RANKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard 10-Mark Problem (Repeated in Jun 2025 Q.2b, Nov 2025 Q.2b, Dec 2024 Q.2a)

Sample Problem Template:
  Collection C:
    d1: "beautiful garden"
    d2: "evening garden time"
    d3: "garden time is beautiful"
  Query:
    q: "garden time"

Step-by-Step Execution Blueprint:
  1. Extract Vocabulary V: {beautiful, evening, garden, is, time} (Total N = 3 documents).
  2. Document Term Frequency (TF) Table:
       Term      | d1 | d2 | d3 | q | Document Freq (df) | IDF = log10(N/df)
       ----------+----+----+----+---+--------------------+-------------------
       beautiful |  1 |  0 |  1 | 0 |        2           | log10(3/2) = 0.176
       evening   |  0 |  1 |  0 | 0 |        1           | log10(3/1) = 0.477
       garden    |  1 |  1 |  1 | 1 |        3           | log10(3/3) = 0.000
       is        |  0 |  0 |  1 | 0 |        1           | log10(3/1) = 0.477
       time      |  0 |  1 |  1 | 1 |        2           | log10(3/2) = 0.176
  3. Compute TF-IDF Weight Vectors: w_{t,d} = tf_{t,d} * idf_t.
  4. Compute Query Vector: w_{t,q} = tf_{t,q} * idf_t.
  5. Compute Dot Products (q . d1), (q . d2), (q . d3).
  6. Compute Magnitudes ||q||, ||d1||, ||d2||, ||d3||.
  7. Compute Cosine Score = (q . d) / (||q|| * ||d||).
  8. Rank Documents in descending order of Cosine Score.

ALGORITHMIC TRACE: BOYER-MOORE BAD MATCH (BAD CHARACTER) TABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard 10-Mark Problem (Repeated in Jun 2025 Q.4b)

Given:
  Pattern P: "SMART" (Length m = 5)
  Alphabet: All characters in alphabet Sigma.

Bad Character Rule Shift Formula:
  Shift(c) = m - 1 - last_occurrence_index(c) for c in Pattern
  Shift(c) = m for any character not in Pattern

Bad Match Table for "SMART" (Indices: S=0, M=1, A=2, R=3, T=4):
  • 'S': 5 - 1 - 0 = 4
  • 'M': 5 - 1 - 1 = 3
  • 'A': 5 - 1 - 2 = 2
  • 'R': 5 - 1 - 3 = 1
  • 'T': 5 - 1 - 4 = 0 (or m = 5 if looking past match)
  • Any other character *: 5

Matching Execution:
  Align Pattern with String; compare right-to-left.
  Upon mismatch at character 'c', shift pattern right by max(1, Shift(c)).
